FAQ

Questions live sellers ask

Can I try it for free?
Yes — create an account and start selling for free, no credit card needed. Upgrade anytime when your lives grow.
Which platforms does it work with?
TikTok LIVE and Facebook Live. Connect your account once and the app captures comments automatically each time you go live.
What printers are supported?
Bluetooth thermal sticker printers (like AIMO) and LAN receipt printers (like Xprinter). Chinese buyer names print correctly.
How does 7-11 shipping work?
Encode your buyers' pickup details in the app, export one Excel file, upload it to 賣貨便, and print all your waybills with one ibon scan.
How do I pay for a plan?
Via Wise transfer — message our Telegram and your plan is activated within minutes. No app-store fees, no hidden charges.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Plans are month-to-month. If you stop, your account simply returns to the free tier — your data stays.
