The 6 things (details in Table 2):

2. **Automatic receipt sent in the middle of a live**, with only part of the items. *Or keep automatic receipts off for the test.*

6. **Testers see dead "Facebook page 1" boxes** on Settings → Facebook, above the real Pages button.

Before the Render deploy of Build 10/10b:

- **A broken request shows a full program trace** to anyone (no error handler).

## Picture sizes (hat 5)

- **Product picture:**
  - ≤ 800 px longest side, JPEG quality 0.8 → 0.5. The first result ≤ 150 KB wins; refused if over 400 KB.
  - The bucket accepts at most 409,600 bytes, JPEG/PNG/WEBP only.
  - The grid tile (~160×70 px) downloads the full file; browser cache 5 min (M16).
- **Receipt picture** (shared layout `src/lib/receiptLayout.js`):
  - Width 720 px. Fonts: items 30 px, total 36 px, header 38 px.
  - At most 400 lines, then "…and N more". At most 12,000 px tall. With a QR, about 245 one-line items fit.
  - Limits: phone 3 MB, server 4 MB.
  - Long CJK names wrap and are never clipped.
  - Readable in Messenger for short orders. Over ~30 lines it needs pinch-zoom (check on a real phone).
  - Emoji draw as empty boxes on the automatic receipt (L17).
- **QR in the receipt:**
  - Upload: ≤ 600 px, never enlarged, ≤ ~220 KB.
  - Drawn at most 420 px wide. A 150 px QR stays 150 px: sharp, but small in Messenger.
  - The server refuses over 2 MB or 1,000 px, but decodes first (M9).
- **Sticker QR:** none for Facebook and Instagram buyers (printing.fbNoQr.test.ts). Shopee wrongly gets one (L16).
