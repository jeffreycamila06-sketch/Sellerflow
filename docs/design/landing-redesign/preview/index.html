How a live works, step by step

You keep selling the way you sell today. The app takes over the notebook.

  1. Go live as usual

    Start your live in TikTok, Facebook or Shopee. Nothing changes for your viewers.

  2. Open SellerFlowLive and tap Connect

    Pick your account, then choose how long this sale runs: 1 day, or up to 5 days if you ship at the end. Buyer numbers stay the same for the whole session.

  3. Comments arrive on the Live screen

    Every comment shows with the buyer's name and picture. Comments that look like a claim are marked MINE so you spot them fast.

  4. Tap the comment to make the order

    1-Click makes the order at once. Type price lets you type the amount first. You can also set one price for everything, pick from your own price buttons, or let Auto mode take orders from product codes.

  5. The sticker prints

    Buyer number in big digits, name, username, the comment or the price, date and time. Stick it on the item or the bag. The same buyer keeps the same number, so their items end up in one bag.

  6. After the live

    Orders lists every buyer with their total. Send the buyer their order summary on TikTok, or a receipt picture on Messenger. Sales shows your totals, top buyers and best sellers.

Questions sellers ask

Is it really free to start?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 1 live account and 150 orders in total. You do not need a card. When you want unlimited orders, move to a paid plan.

Do my buyers need to install anything?

No. Buyers comment on your live as they always do.

Do I have to change how I go live?

No. You go live in TikTok, Facebook or Shopee as usual, then tap Connect in SellerFlowLive.

Which printer should I buy?

For stickers, the AIMO or Phomemo D520BT is the standard choice. The app also works with the Xprinter XP-420B, Flash Y41BT and Niimbot B1, B21, B203 and B3S. For paper slips, a WiFi receipt printer. See the list above.

Can I use it without a printer?

Yes. Orders, buyer numbers and totals work without one. You can add a printer later.

What if two buyers claim the same item?

You decide who gets it: you tap the comment you accept. With Auto mode, stock goes down with each order and stops at zero.

My sale runs for three days. Do buyer numbers start again each day?

No. Choose a session of up to 5 days and each buyer keeps one number for all of it.

Does it print Chinese names?

Yes. Chinese, Thai, Vietnamese, Japanese and Korean names print as written.

How do I pay for a plan?

Message us on Telegram @SellerFlowLive. We tell you how to pay and turn your plan on. Plans run month to month.

What happens when my plan ends?

You can still open the app and see your orders and sales. To go live and print again, renew.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, from Settings in the app, at any time.
