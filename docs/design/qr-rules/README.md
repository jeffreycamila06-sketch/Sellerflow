# QR rules on the new sticker (Jeff 2026-11-10)

One picture per printer: rows 50×30, 50×40, 60×40, 40×30; left = TODAY (main), right = NEW. Each sticker is what
the printer RECEIVES (decoded from the real bytes), drawn on its label (Niimbot: the SDK frame sits 1 mm inside the
label). Red line = 2 mm from the right edge. Sample: Buyer 8888, @maria_live, QR on.

- TODAY: the QR is 8 dots (1 mm) from the right edge on every printer; a QR prints on 40×30.
- NEW: the QR is 16 dots (2 mm) from the right edge; no QR below 50×30. On 50×40 the QR module is 4 dots (was 5).
- Niimbot B3S: already 2.5 mm from the label edge; only the 40×30 QR goes.
