Delete Your SellerFlowLive Account

SellerFlowLive · Last updated: 6 June 2026

This page explains how to delete your SellerFlowLive account and what happens to your data. SellerFlowLive is a business tool for live-selling sellers.

1. How to Delete Your Account in the App

If you are logged in, you can delete your account directly:

  • Open the SellerFlowLive app or website and sign in.
  • Open your Profile menu.
  • Tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm by typing your email address.

This permanently deletes your account immediately.

2. How to Request Deletion by Contact

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by contacting us by phone at 0958704782. Provide your registered email address and tell us you wish to delete your account. We will process your request within 30 days.

3. What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your account and login information (email).
  • Your stored orders and customer records.
  • Your store profile and settings.

4. What Data May Be Kept

We may retain limited records where required by law (for example, basic transaction logs) for up to 90 days, after which they are also deleted. We do not keep your data longer than necessary.
