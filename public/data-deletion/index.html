Data Deletion Instructions

SellerFlowLive · Last updated: October 9, 2026

How to delete your data from SellerFlowLive, operated by SELLERFLOWLIVE PRINTER TRADING. See also our Privacy Policy.

Sellers

Delete your account and all its data yourself, anytime:

  • Sign in to the SellerFlowLive app or website.
  • Open Settings.
  • Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account, orders, customers and settings are removed permanently.

If you remove SellerFlowLive in Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites, your connected Pages are removed from SellerFlowLive too.

Can't sign in? Email jeffreycamila06@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and your registered email. We respond within 30 days.

Facebook users

If you commented on a seller's Facebook live video:

  • Remove our access: Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites → remove SellerFlowLive.
  • Delete your comment data: email jeffreycamila06@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and your Facebook name or username. We respond within 30 days.
