Data Deletion Instructions

SellerFlowLive · Last updated: September 23, 2026

This page explains how to delete data associated with SellerFlowLive, operated by SELLERFLOWLIVE PRINTER TRADING. It covers both sellers who have an account and Facebook users whose public comments were processed through the app. For how we handle data generally, see our Privacy Policy.

1. Sellers — Delete Your Account and Data

If you have a SellerFlowLive seller account, you can delete everything yourself:

  • Sign in to the SellerFlowLive app or website.
  • Open Settings.
  • Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This permanently removes your profile (email, store name, phone, saved platform usernames), your orders, and your saved customers.

If you cannot sign in, email jeffreycamila06@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and your registered email address. We process these requests within 30 days.

2. Facebook Users and Commenters

If your public comment on a seller's Facebook Live was read by SellerFlowLive, note that this comment data is purged automatically on a rolling basis (see the Privacy Policy). You can also act directly:

  • Stop all access immediately: remove SellerFlowLive in Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites. Once removed, the app can no longer receive any data about your interactions.
  • Request deletion of comment data already processed: email jeffreycamila06@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and your Facebook profile name or handle so we can locate and remove any related data. We process these requests within 30 days.

3. What Happens After Deletion

Once processed, the requested data is permanently removed from our systems. Some information may persist briefly in encrypted backups before being overwritten on the normal backup cycle, and we may retain the minimum records required by law.
