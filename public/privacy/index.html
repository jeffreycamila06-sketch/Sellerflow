Privacy Policy

SellerFlowLive · Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SELLERFLOWLIVE PRINTER TRADING ("SellerFlowLive", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the SellerFlowLive application and the website at sellerflowlive.com. By using SellerFlowLive, you agree to the practices described below.

1. What SellerFlowLive Is

SellerFlowLive is a live-selling order-management tool for sellers. It helps a seller read the live comments from their own live stream on platforms such as TikTok, Facebook, and Shopee, turn those comments into orders, manage customers and products, and produce shipping labels. It is a business tool operated by sellers, not a consumer social product.

2. Information We Collect From Sellers

When a seller creates an account and uses the app, we collect only what is needed to run the service:

  • Account details — email address, store name, phone number, and a securely managed password (authentication is handled by Supabase; passwords are stored hashed and are never visible to us).
  • Saved platform usernames — the TikTok / Facebook handles a seller saves so the app knows which live session to read.
  • Products and orders — the product list, prices, and the orders a seller creates during a live session.
  • Buyer and parcel details a seller enters for shipping — recipient name, phone number, and 7-ELEVEN pickup store, used only to generate the seller's shipping labels.

3. Live-Stream Data Processed Through Platform APIs

When a seller connects a live session, SellerFlowLive receives that session's public comment data through the platform's official APIs — including the Facebook Live Video API. This data may include a commenter's display name, username/handle, profile picture (avatar), comment text, and timestamps.

This live-comment data is used solely to let the seller see comments and create orders from them. It is never sold and is never used for advertising or any purpose unrelated to the seller's own live-selling operation.

4. How We Use Information

  • To let sellers log in securely and access only their own account data.
  • To display live comments from a seller's connected session and let them create orders from those comments.
  • To store and manage the seller's orders, customers, and products.
  • To generate shipping labels from the details the seller enters.
  • To keep the service secure and reliable.

5. Payments

SellerFlowLive does not process payments. Any payment between a seller and their buyers, or any subscription for the app, happens outside the app; we do not collect or store card or bank details.

6. Storage and Security

Data is stored in Supabase (a hosted PostgreSQL database). Access is controlled per seller account using row-level security, so each seller can access only their own data. Connections are encrypted and access is protected by secure authentication tokens. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to safeguard information.

7. Data Retention

We keep only what we need for as long as we need it:

  • Live comment and order-feed data is purged automatically on a rolling basis — order records used for the live session are removed after roughly 10 days.
  • Account data (email, store profile, saved usernames, products) is kept while the account is active and is removed when the account is deleted.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with the providers that make the service work — Supabase (authentication and database) and our hosting providers (such as Vercel and Render) — and with TikTok, Facebook, or Shopee only as needed to read a live session the seller has connected. We may disclose information if required by law.

9. Meta Platform Data

Any data obtained through Meta (Facebook) APIs is used in accordance with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies, only for the live-comment ordering purpose described above. A Facebook user can revoke SellerFlowLive's access at any time in Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.

10. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your account and data at any time. See our Data Deletion Instructions for how sellers and Facebook users can request deletion.

11. Children's Privacy

SellerFlowLive is a business tool intended for sellers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
